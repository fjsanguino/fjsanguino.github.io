About me


My name is Javier Sanguino Bautiste, I am a PhD student in the Basque Center of Applied Mathematics (BCAM) under the supervision of Novi Quadrianto and Jose A. Lozano.

My main research interests are Performative Prediction and fair ML. I'm also very interested in spurious correlations in Deep Learning.

Previous to that I received my BSc from the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM) and my MSc from ETH Zurich, thanks to the generous help of the Fundación La Caixa . In Zürich, I closely collaborated with the IVIA Lab (led by Prof. Menna El-Assady) in interactive human-AI collaboration and I did my masters thesis in the Data Analytics Lab (led by Prof. Thomas Hofman) in Deep Learning Theory.

In my free time, I (try to) surf 🏄‍♂️ and I coach basketball 🏀.


Contact: jsanguino AT bcamath DOT org
